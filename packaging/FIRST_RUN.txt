MacTR v1.4.0 — First launch
================================

1. Drag MacTR.app to Applications.
2. Because this community build is not notarized, Control-click or right-click
   MacTR.app and choose Open the first time.
3. MacTR then stays in the menu bar. Use its display icon to open Settings,
   enable Launch at Login, pause output, or configure the daily schedule.

No Homebrew, Swift installation, or separate libusb installation is required.

Requirements: Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 15 Sequoia or newer.
